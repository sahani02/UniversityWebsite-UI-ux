What criteria do we use?

Under the admission policies in operation at present the candidates who have obtained at least "S" grade in three of the approved subjects in the respective stream (Art,Commerce and Science) and a minimum of 30% for the Common General Paper are eligible to apply for admission to a university.

 

Admission Process

Candidates should apply online for university admission via the official website of the

UGC (www.ug.ac.lk) and forward the duly signed printed copy of online generated

application from to the UGC via registered post along with the other relavent documents

on or before the deadline given for application as pronounced by the press notices.
